Course: Elective VI A — Enterprise Systems

Smart Book Review Management & Customer Opinion Mining System

Architecture, ERP Modularity, 3-Tier MVC, and Role-Based Access Control

Abstract

This enterprise platform integrates a 3-tier Model-View-Controller (MVC) architecture with Service-Oriented Architecture (SOA) principles to provide comprehensive literature inventory and customer opinion intelligence. Built using a normalized SQLite relational data tier, an asynchronous FastAPI application logic tier, and a responsive glassmorphic Tailwind presentation tier, the system enables segregated capabilities for Administrators and Customers while providing cryptographic audit trails and ERP master catalog management.

Chapter 1: Introduction & Enterprise Scope

Defines the organizational motivation to transform raw consumer book feedback into actionable CRM intelligence. Incorporates loose coupling, open REST contracts, and asynchronous client communication.

Chapter 2: ERP Modularity & System Tiers

Maps enterprise modules: Materials Management (Book Catalog), Sales & CRM (Customer Feedback & CSAT), Security Subsystem (RBAC with SHA-256 password salting and digital audit signatures).

Chapter 3: Implementation & Database Architecture

Relational schema design with 36 titles across 35 distinct genres, foreign-key constraints on customer reviews, transactional ACID guarantees, and automated metrics recalculation on every review submission.

Chapter 4: Results & System Screenshots

Presents Figure 5.1 (Product-wise Sentiment Analysis bar chart), Figure 5.2 (Aspect Frequency bar chart), and Figure 5.3 (Stored Reviews Table) verified under both Admin and Customer roles.

Course: Elective IV B — Advanced Social, Text and Media Analytics

Aspect-Based Opinion Mining & Lexicon Sentiment Intelligence

VADER Scoring, 10 Domain Literary Aspects, RAKE Keyphrases, and Temporal Polarization

Abstract

Applies multi-stage natural language processing (NLP) pipelines to unstructured literary reviews from Kaggle, Amazon, and Goodreads. Preprocessing includes lowercasing, regex cleaning, tokenization, stopword removal, and morphological reduction. Aspect-based sentiment analysis scores 10 literary dimensions (Plot & Story, Writing Style, Character Development, Pacing, Emotional Depth, Worldbuilding, Dialogue, Ending & Climax, Originality & Concept, Price & Value) while Rapid Automatic Keyword Extraction (RAKE) calculates token co-occurrence degree-to-frequency ratios.

Unit I & II: Text Mining & Sentiment Modeling

VADER compound polarity $[-1.0, +1.0]$, subjective confidence thresholds, emotion classification (Joy, Surprise, Sadness, Anger, Fear), and real-time client typing inference.

Unit III: Keyword Extraction & Evaluation

Implementation of RAKE $deg(w)/freq(w)$ candidate scoring and supervised Scikit-Learn TF-IDF + Multinomial Naive Bayes benchmark with Precision, Recall, F1, and Confusion Matrix.

Unit IV & V: Web Clickstream & Network Analysis

Clickstream funnel tracking with randomized A/B testing simulator and bipartite reader-book social graph with degree centrality calculation $C_d(v) = deg(v)/(N-1)$.

Unit VI: Unsupervised Topic Detection (K-Means)

$K=4$ cluster centroids in sparse TF-IDF vector space, automated top keyword extraction per centroid, and Apriori association rule mining ($Support, Confidence, Lift$).
